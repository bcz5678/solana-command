$SPCX

SpaceX was listed on the Nasdaq under the ticker symbol $SPCX on June 12, 2026. The company intended to raise $75 billion by selling 555.6 million shares at $135 apiece, targeting a mind-boggling valuation of roughly $1.75 trillion. However, after a day of trading the stock now values the company at $2.1 trillion.

A non-traditional offering

Unlike standard IPOs where financial institutions dominate, SpaceX is offering individual investors up to 30% of the deal at the same price. Brokerages like Robinhood, Fidelity, and SoFi have lowered their minimum participation requirements to as little as $2,000.

Because of the listing's enormous scale, the Nasdaq-100 has created a new fast-entry rule allowing SpaceX to join the benchmark after just 15 days of trading.
